Candidate Portal

A shareable, no-login careers page bundled with the recruiter app. Candidates browse open roles, apply in under a minute, and track their applications with a private link.

What candidates can do

  • Browse open roles at #/portal
  • One-click apply — details, screening questions, and EEO self-identification
  • Resume auto-fill — upload a .pdf, .docx, or .txt; contact, skills, and summary are extracted locally (best-effort)
  • Status tracking — a private link (#/portal/status/<token>) shows where the application sits in the pipeline
  • Manage my data — export a portable JSON copy (plus resume) with a proof token, or request full erasure (DSAR)

Consent & privacy

  • Applications record explicit consent with a timestamp and policy version; non-consenting applications are rejected (422).
  • EEO / self-identification answers (Workday/OFCCP-style, with per-question disclosure) are stored for compliance but are never shown in any recruiter pipeline view.
  • Screening answers are shown to recruiters (labelled with the question) and are not shown to other candidates.

What candidates cannot see

  • Match scores are never exposed — the portal shows stage status only.
  • Internal company fields and recruiter application details are stripped from the public job routes.

Under the hood (public routes)

See the API reference for the full list of unauthenticated /api/public/* endpoints — jobs, company brand, resume parse, applications, privacy, export, and erasure.
