The Recruiter App

The recruiter UI is designed to feel and behave like LinkedIn Recruiter — reworking the classic flat screens into Recruiter-style flows while keeping all matching, screening, and branding data intact.

App shell & navigation

  • Top bar — company logo (with a frosted-glass backdrop), the wordmark "Recruiter", a global search, a link to the candidate portal, the current recruiter, org settings (admin only), a dark-mode toggle, and log out.
  • Icon rail (desktop) — a narrow 76px left rail: Home, Candidates, My Jobs, Projects, then a spacer and the tools group (Screening and Activity log).
  • Mobile — the rail collapses into a drawer.

Candidates search

A filter panel (Keywords, Job title, Location, Must-have skills, Min/Max years, Status) plus result rows showing avatar, name, headline, location·years, skill pills, and actions (Open profile / Edit / Archive–Reactivate). Archiving replaces delete in the search UI.

Candidate profile

Clicking a candidate opens a right slide-in drawer:

  • A header card with avatar, name, title, location/contact, experience, and a stage chip.
  • Add to project opens a dialog listing open jobs.
  • Edit and Archive actions.
  • Tabs for About, Applications, and Notes.
  • Each application row has a stage menu (pipeline stages) and a notes editor.

My Jobs

A list of jobs with status chips (All / Open / Closed), each row showing its pipeline stage counts and actions: Pipeline, Find candidates, Questions, Edit, and Delete. Selecting a job opens a detail drawer; "Pipeline" deep-links to #/projects?job=<id>.

Projects pipeline

The Projects view is a kanban board with five stages mapped onto the existing applications.status values — no schema changes needed:

Columnstatus
Candidatesmatched
Reached outin_review
Interviewinginterview
Hiredhired
Rejectedrejected

A job selector on the left filters the board. Cards show candidate name, title, and location, with move-left / move-right chevrons that call updateApplicationStatus. Clicking a card opens the candidate profile. Columns are fluid (flex: 1 1 0, minWidth: 185, maxWidth: 264, overflowX: auto) to avoid stray scrollbars.

Branding & themes

  • Adopt a company in Organization settings (admin only): the app fetches the real logo from the web, derives a brand color, sets the org title, and builds a custom light & dark theme around it. Adoption is all-or-nothing — if the logo can't be downloaded or a color can't be derived, nothing changes.
  • Dark mode is neutral gray (not blue) by default.
  • Nav, accent, and KPI colors derive from the brand color client-side.
Note: the search dropdown renders as a plain rectangle, and the top-bar logo is shown as a raw image with a frosted-glass band behind a square-ish "Recruiter" wordmark — even swaps between companies with different logo aspect ratios keep the same footprint.
